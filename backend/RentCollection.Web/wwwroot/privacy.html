Privacy Policy

Alrtam mobile application · Last updated: 8 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ALRTAM Company ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Alrtam mobile application (the "App"). The App lets tenants view their leases, invoices and payment history, pay rent, submit maintenance requests, and receive notifications from their property manager.

1. Information We Collect

  • Identity & contact details — your name, Civil ID (Kuwait national ID number), phone number and email address, provided by your property manager and used to sign you in and identify your tenancy.
  • Tenancy & financial information — your lease details, invoices, rent amounts, payment history and any legal-case status relating to your tenancy.
  • Payment information — when you pay rent, your payment is processed by the KNET payment gateway. Card/bank details are entered on KNET's secure pages; we do not receive or store your full card number. We store the payment result, amount, and a transaction reference.
  • Photos — if you attach a photo to a maintenance request, we access your camera or photo library only for that upload, with your action.
  • Device & push token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send you notifications (e.g. payment reminders, maintenance updates).
  • Technical & log data — basic information such as app version and error logs used to keep the App working and secure.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and show your leases, invoices and payments.
  • To process rent and other payments through KNET.
  • To receive and manage your maintenance requests.
  • To send you notifications relevant to your tenancy.
  • To secure the service, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Your property manager / landlord, who operates the tenancy the App serves.
  • KNET and its banking partners, to process your payments.
  • Google Firebase, to deliver push notifications.
  • Service providers that host our systems, and authorities where required by law.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your tenancy is active and thereafter as required for legal, accounting and dispute-resolution purposes. When no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and access is restricted. While no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and contractual limits. To make a request, contact us using the details below or speak to your property manager.

7. Children

The App is intended for adult tenants and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
