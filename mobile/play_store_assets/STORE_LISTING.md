**Full description:**
```
Alrtam is the official tenant app for properties managed on the Alrtam platform. Sign in securely with your Civil ID to manage your tenancy from your phone — anytime, anywhere.

Features:
• View your active leases and property details
• See your invoices and any outstanding rent at a glance
• Pay rent securely online through KNET
• Track your complete payment history
• Submit maintenance requests, with photos
• Stay informed about your case and hearing status
• Receive notifications for payments, reminders and updates
• Full English and Arabic support, with right-to-left layout

Alrtam keeps you connected with your property manager and makes renting simpler, clearer and paperless.
```
